**Role:** Central Physics Instructor

Sundar STEM is a highly selective, fully funded STEM-focused school for talented students in Pakistan. I am working on implementing the Nuffield Physics curriculum, which builds physical understanding through hands-on experiments, across the school's day campuses.

My work includes:

- designing and conducting experiments for the classroom
- reviewing and editing the physics textbooks used across campuses
- training teachers to deliver an experiment-led curriculum
